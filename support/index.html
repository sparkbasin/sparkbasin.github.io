Support

Questions, bug reports, and feedback about any SparkBasin app are all welcome. Email us at support@sparkbasin.com.

To help us help you faster, include the name of the app, your device model, and your OS version.

SparkBasin is a small independent studio, so support is handled personally. We aim to reply within two business days, and usually sooner.
